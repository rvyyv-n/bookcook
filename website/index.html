A cookbook you can talk to.

Keep the recipes that only live in someone’s head. Say them out loud, the way you’d tell a friend, and Bookcook writes them down. Then cook from them with your hands free.

Tell it the way you’d tell a friend.

Bookcook asks one question at a time and writes down what you say. Ingredients land in the right place as you say them, and times become timers. You check it all before it’s saved.

  1. What’s this dish called?
  2. Whose recipe is this?
  3. How many people does it feed?
  4. Tell me the ingredients, one at a time.
  5. Now the steps. What do you do first?
  6. Any tips for someone making this?
  7. Who taught you this?

      On the phone, the tablet and the computer.

      Yours, and only yours.

      • No account to make.
      • No server holding your recipes.
      • No AI, and no paid services.
      • No ads, no tracking.
      • No streaks, no nagging.

      It all lives on your device.

      Recipes, photos and voice notes stay on your phone or computer, not on someone else’s.

      Share it as a link.

      The recipe travels inside the link itself. Whoever opens it can add it to their own Bookcook.

      Back it all up.

      Save everything to a file you keep, photos and voice notes included.
