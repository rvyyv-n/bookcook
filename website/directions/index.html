Three directions for the website

Each one is the opening screen and one working section, at phone width. They all use the app’s own colours, type, logo and recipe reader, so what they show is what the app does. Pick one and we’ll design the full page in that direction.

A. Say it

Quiet Page. The headline is written down as it’s said.

Calm and editorial. The try-it section is the Tell it screen: speak or type an ingredient and it’s sorted into amount, ingredient and note.

Open on its own

B. Hands free

Colour field. The page opens on the tomato of cook mode.

Bold and loud. The try-it section is cook mode itself: real steps, real timers you start by tapping a time, Read, and listening for “next”.

Open on its own

C. In her words

Letterpress Heirloom. A page from the family cookbook.

Warm and personal, led by Mom’s story. The try-it section grows the whole page to Huge, and a servings ruler rescales the recipe as you drag it.
