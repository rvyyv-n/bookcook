A round of polish that makes Bookcook feel smoother to use.

Install instructions are in the [first release notes](v1.0.0.md) and the README. If you already have the app, it will offer this update.

## What's new

- **Smoother movement.** Screens slide in and out, and a recipe card's photo grows into the recipe when you open it.
- **Sheets are easier to close.** Drag a sheet down to dismiss it, and they now stay clear of the on-screen keyboard.
- **Better touch feedback.** Buttons and cards respond more clearly when you tap them.
- **A tidier recipe page on phones**, with Metric / Imperial sitting beside the Ingredients heading.
- **The example collection is now called Weeknights.**
- **About section in Settings**, showing the version you have and links to the project on GitHub.

## Windows app

There's now a Windows installer, `bookcook-1.1.0-setup.exe`, at the bottom of this page. Run it to install Bookcook as its own app: it opens in its own window, works offline and tells you when a new version is out. It isn't signed yet, so Windows may say **Windows protected your PC**: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It installs just for you, with no administrator needed. Your recipes stay on the computer you saved them on; use **Settings → Back up now** and **Restore** to move them.
