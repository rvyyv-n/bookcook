Bookcook now has its own website, a place to send people who haven't tried it yet.

Install instructions are in the [first release notes](v1.0.0.md) and the README. The app itself is the same as 1.1.0, so there's nothing new to learn.

## What's new

- **A website at [getbookcook.pages.dev](https://getbookcook.pages.dev/).** It tells Bookcook's story and lets people try the real thing before they open the app: watch Mom's biryani being written down as she talks, type or say an ingredient and see how it's understood, step through cook mode with real timers, and switch between the five looks, light and dark, and the three text sizes.
- **Made for phones as well as computers.** On a phone the page is shorter and tidier, the demos fit the screen at every text size, and Open the app stays within reach at the bottom.
- **Big text there too.** The website has the same Normal, Large and Huge text sizes as the app.
