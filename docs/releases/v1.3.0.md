A polish release: a rearranged Settings page, empty pages that show what they're for, and smoother movement throughout. Nothing you've saved changes: your recipes, settings and backups carry over as they are.

## Getting it

- **Already installed?** Bookcook offers the update itself, the next time you open it or within the hour (never while you're cooking). You can also go to **Settings → App → Check for updates**.
- **Android app.** Download `bookcook-1.3.0.apk` from the bottom of this page and install it over the one you have.
- **Windows app.** Download `bookcook-1.3.0-setup.exe` from the bottom of this page and run it. It isn't signed yet, so Windows may say **Windows protected your PC**: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **On the web, iPhone and Mac.** Open [bookcook.pages.dev](https://bookcook.pages.dev/); it's already on 1.3.0.

New to Bookcook? The full install steps for every device are in the [README](https://github.com/rvyyv-n/bookcook#install).

## What's new

### Settings, rearranged

- Each group of settings sits on its own card, in the order you reach for them: Text size, Look, Voice, About you, Your cookbook and App.
- The backup reminder is no longer stuck at the top of the page. It's under **Your cookbook**, beside **Print the family cookbook**, and still stands out in your accent colour when a backup is overdue.
- **Voice folds away.** Tap **Voice** to open reading aloud, speech speed and the voice, and tap again to fold it shut.
- On a computer, a short index with icons sits beside the page and jumps to each group, showing which one you're in as you scroll.

### Empty pages that explain themselves

A new cookbook, an empty grocery list and no requests yet each show a sample of what goes there, how it works in three steps, and the one thing to do next:

- **Cookbook:** say it, we write it, cook from it, with **Let's save your first recipe** and **Try an example**.
- **Grocery:** pick a recipe, add to grocery, shop by aisle, with a way back to the cookbook.
- **Requests:** you ask, they tell it, you keep it, with **Send request**.

### Smoother to use

- Choices like text size, theme and speech speed slide to what you pick instead of jumping.
- Folds, like Voice in Settings and In her words on a recipe, fade their content in as they open.
- Recipe cards, collections, requests and grocery aisles rise in one after another the first time you open them. Coming back to a page, they're simply there.
- Buttons and tabs give way the moment you press them, and everything moves at the same pace and ease.
- **Reduce Motion** on your device still turns all of it off.

### Small fixes

- The greeting reads **Good Morning**, **Good Afternoon** or **Good Evening**.
- The highlight on a settings row, like **Style** while its list is open, is rounded to match everything else.
- The text-size preview keeps its card shape in the Spice Tin and Colour-coded looks.

## Known issue

**Printing from the iPhone Home Screen app.** Tapping **Print** in **Print the family cookbook** may do nothing on some iPhones when Bookcook is opened from its Home Screen icon. A fix is planned for the next release.
