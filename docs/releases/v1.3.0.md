A tidier Bookcook: a rearranged Settings page, empty pages that show what they're for, and smoother movement throughout.

Install instructions are in the [first release notes](v1.0.0.md) and the README. Your recipes, settings and backups carry over as they are.

## What's new

- **Settings, rearranged.** Each group of settings sits on its own card, in the order you reach for them. The backup reminder is no longer stuck at the top: it's under Your cookbook, beside Print the family cookbook. On a computer, a short index with icons beside the page jumps to each group.
- **Voice folds away.** Tap Voice in Settings to open reading aloud, speech speed and the voice, and tap again to fold it shut.
- **Empty pages that explain themselves.** A new cookbook, an empty grocery list and no requests yet each show a sample of what goes there and how it works in three steps.
- **Smoother to use.** Choices like text size slide to what you pick, folds fade open, recipe cards rise in when you first open the app, and buttons give way the moment you press them. Reduce Motion still turns it all off.
- **Small fixes.** The greeting reads Good Morning, Good Afternoon or Good Evening. The highlight on a settings row, like Style while its list is open, is rounded to match everything else.
