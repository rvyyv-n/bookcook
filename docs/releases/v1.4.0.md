Sharing that sends the whole recipe, a recipe page that fits each look, and movement that feels calm and finished. Nothing you've saved changes: your recipes, settings and backups carry over as they are.

## Getting it

- **Already installed?** Bookcook offers the update itself, the next time you open it or within the hour (never while you're cooking). You can also go to **Settings → App → Check for updates**.
- **Android app.** Download `bookcook-1.4.0.apk` from the bottom of this page and install it over the one you have.
- **Windows app.** Download `bookcook-1.4.0-setup.exe` from the bottom of this page and run it. It isn't signed yet, so Windows may say **Windows protected your PC**: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **On the web, iPhone and Mac.** Open [bookcook.pages.dev](https://bookcook.pages.dev/); it's already on 1.4.0.

New to Bookcook? The full install steps for every device are in the [README](https://github.com/rvyyv-n/bookcook#install).

## What's new

### Sharing, three ways

**Share** on a recipe now asks how:

- **Send a Bookcook link.** They add it to their cookbook in one tap. Opening it shows the whole recipe first: the story, every ingredient under its heading and, a tap away, the steps.
- **Send as text.** The whole recipe in the message, with its photo, so anyone can read it without Bookcook.
- **Copy link.** To paste wherever you like.

Voice notes always stay on your phone.

### The recipe page

- **Buttons that suit each look.** Quiet puts Edit, Make Mine, Grocery and Share on one soft card. Heirloom lays them out like the contents page of an old cookbook. Spice Tin, Colour-coded and Colour field give each one a round button in a tray.
- **Fewer buttons on a phone.** Print is on a computer only, and **Add to grocery** is now just **Grocery**.
- **A tidy end to the page.** Collections, other versions and the source sit together on one card, and **Delete recipe** is on its own below it.

### Settings

- **Reading aloud** folds away inside the Voice card and shows whether it's on and how fast it reads.
- **Back up now** and **Restore** are plain rows under **Your cookbook**, with when you last backed up beside them.

### Smaller things

- **Grocery:** when everything on the list comes from one recipe, the header says so once instead of under every item. The **Add** button lines up with the field.
- **Editing:** "From **Mom**'s kitchen" no longer has a gap before the 's. On a phone, the section tabs fade at the edge when there are more, and the one you pick slides into view.
- **Cooking:** messages like "Added 13 items to your grocery list" sit above Back, Read and Next instead of on top of them.
- **Typing in a sheet:** the sheet makes room for the keyboard, so the button you need stays in reach.

### Calm, finished movement

- **Save recipe** turns into a tick, then your recipe opens.
- **Ticking an item** off the grocery list or while cooking: the box pops and the tick draws itself in.
- **Changing servings:** the amounts roll up when there are more and down when there are fewer.
- **Cooking progress:** the bar at the top fills into the next step.
- **Lists settle:** remove a request or put a recipe back with Undo, and the cards around it glide into place. Sorting the cookbook does the same.
- **Photos** fade in once they've loaded instead of popping in.
- **The tab bar:** the tab you open lifts into place, and the **+** button presses in.
- **Messages** can be swiped away with your finger.

**Reduce Motion** on your device still turns all of it off.

## Known issue

**Printing from the iPhone Home Screen app.** Tapping **Print** in **Print the family cookbook** may do nothing on some iPhones when Bookcook is opened from its Home Screen icon. A fix is planned.
