  1. Every skin passes your targets in light and dark. Ink is 13.3:1 or more, and muted is 6.7:1 or more, so muted clears AAA as well.
  2. Saffron used as a mark (progress, pulse, rings, underlines) is only about 2:1 on light paper. Fix: the new --accent-mark, #B8791A in light saffron, which is 3.1–3.4:1. Saffron fills keep #E9A23B.
  3. Control borders in --line-strong are 1.7–2.2:1. That's fine on buttons, which have text labels, but too faint to find a text field or checkbox. Fix: the new --line-control, 3.2–3.7:1 in light and 3.4:1 in dark.
  4. White on tomato is 5.7:1. That passes AA, which is the brief's bar for accent-ink, but not AAA. Labels are bold 18px or larger. #9E3521 would reach 7:1, but it reads as brick rather than tomato, so I kept your value. Your call.

Rules

Accessibility

  • Body text is 18px or larger, and ink is 7:1 or more. Muted text is 4.5:1 or more and is never used for anything you must read to act.
  • Tap targets are 56px or more (tab items 64px). Rows are targets as a whole.
  • Every icon has a visible label. There are no icon-only buttons.
  • There are no hidden gestures. Every swipe or drag has a button (Move, Delete).
  • Destructive actions happen at once and show an Undo toast. No confirm dialogs.
  • The focus ring is always visible when using the keyboard: 3px --focus, offset 3px.
  • Large and Huge reflow the layout, never shrink or clip it. Test every screen at Huge.
  • Reduced motion keeps every state change and drops the travel and breathing.
  • Colour is never the only signal. Spice colours always sit beside text (the section name) and are off when a recipe has fewer than 2 sections.

Voice

  • Cook mode commands: next, back, repeat, timer, stop. They're listed in the indicator's tooltip and in Settings, never on screen.
  • The indicator is a pulse and one word: Listening, Paused, or Heard “next” (for 1 second).
  • Read speaks the current step. While speaking, Read becomes Stop (aria-pressed). Next stops speech first.
  • Tell it: say “next” after each item and “done” when finished. The helper line says this once, under the question.
  • Live words appear in muted text and turn to ink once they're understood. The newest parsed row is tinted with accent-soft for 1.2s.
  • The mic is always one tap to toggle, never press-and-hold.
  • If speech isn't supported, the mic is hidden and Type it becomes the primary action. If it's denied, show friendly steps and Type instead.
  • Timer alerts are both spoken and shown. Saying “stop” silences them.

Copy

  • Use one-word actions where possible: Save, Edit, Share, Print, Read, Next, Back, Undo, Speak.
  • Name the outcome: “Save recipe”, “Start cooking”, “Tell it”, “I made it”. Never Submit, Proceed, OK or Confirm.
  • Use “Read”, not “Read aloud”, and “Speak”, not “Dictate”.
  • Talk to the cook, warmly and briefly: “We couldn't read that page. Try Paste it instead.”
  • Her words stay hers. Transcripts and stories are never corrected. Only the structured recipe is tidied.
  • Write quantities as she would: “a handful”, “a pinch”, “to taste” are valid amounts.
  • No filler stats, no exclamation marks in the UI, and no emoji in the product.
  • Use sentence case everywhere. Heirloom's small caps come from a token (--heading-case), so the source text stays in sentence case.
